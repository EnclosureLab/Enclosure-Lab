GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before getting started?

Open a section below to learn what to expect, how to get in touch, and more.

What should I expect?

When interested in my service you will message me through the phone number on the flyer given either in person or on the review page, and we will schedule a date for me to quote your property for free. Once a price and date is determined I will show up at the adress which will be sent to me via text. The time that the job will take me greatly varies depending on the size and shape of your yard, and what job you would like to have completed.

How do I contact?

If you wish to contact me about scheduling an appointment text of call (423)-355-8230.

What if I want to cancel?

If you are unsatisfied with the work you may cancel the next appointment three days in advance via text message provided on the flyer on the review page.
